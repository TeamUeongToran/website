Frequently asked questions

Where is my save data stored?

Save data and settings are stored only on your device. Our games have no accounts, and we do not keep your data on our servers. If you delete the game or reset your device, local save data may be lost. If a platform such as Steam offers cloud saves, that is handled by the platform.

Which platforms are supported?

Romantic Cat Ueong is in development. We plan to release it for PC on Steam and for mobile. Platforms and release dates will be announced on this site when they are confirmed.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases and refunds are handled by the store you bought from, so each store's refund policy applies. Please request a refund through Steam, Google Play, or the App Store. We cannot process refunds ourselves.

I found a bug. What should I do?

Please email us with the details described above. Bug reports help us fix problems faster.
