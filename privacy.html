Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 (posted October 29, 2026). The previous version, effective October 27, 2026, applies until then. See "10. Revision history" for what changed. If the English and Korean versions differ, the Korean version applies.

TeamUeongToran ("we", "us") respects the privacy of everyone who plays our games or visits this website (together, the "Service"). We comply with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea.

1. Information we collect

We do not directly collect personal information to provide the Service. There is no account registration, and we do not ask for your name, contact details, or location.

Game save data and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to our servers.

This website does not use cookies, advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and it serves its fonts from our own server rather than from a third party. If you choose a language, the website remembers your choice in your browser's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may process access logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. We do not have access to these logs.

2. Information handled by platforms

Purchases, payments, refunds, downloads, achievements, and cloud saves are handled by the distribution platforms, such as Steam, Google Play, and the App Store. Information processed in those cases is governed by each platform's privacy policy. We only receive information that does not identify you, such as aggregated sales statistics.

3. Email inquiries

Item
Email inquiries
Purpose
To confirm and answer your inquiry
Data
Your email address and the content of your message, including attachments
Retention
Deleted one year after the inquiry is resolved, or kept for the longer period if the law requires it

How we delete it: we delete the email and its attachments from our mailbox and empty the trash.

Security measures: the inquiry mailbox is accessed only by the owner and is protected with two-step verification.

Cookies: we do not install or operate cookies.

4. Sharing, processors and storage abroad

Inquiry emails are stored in Google Gmail (Google LLC, United States). We do not otherwise share your personal information with third parties or entrust its processing to others, except where required by law.

5. Advertising and analytics

The Service currently includes no advertising or usage analytics tools. If we add such features, we will first update this policy to describe the tools used and the information processed.

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete any personal information we hold about you, such as an email inquiry, or ask us to stop processing it. To make a request, email the privacy officer at the address below. We will respond without delay.
