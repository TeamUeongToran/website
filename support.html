Frequently asked questions

Where is my save data stored?

Save data and settings are stored only on your device. Our games have no accounts and no cloud sync, and we do not keep your data on our servers. If you delete the game or reset your device, the save data may be lost.

Can I move my progress to a new device?

No. Because save data stays on one device and is not synced, progress does not carry over when you change devices. Please keep this in mind before you replace or reset your device.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases and refunds are handled by the store you bought from, so each store's refund policy applies. Please request a refund through Steam, Google Play, or the App Store. We cannot process refunds ourselves.

Which devices and operating systems are supported?

Romantic Cat Ueong is in development. We plan to release it for PC (Steam) and for mobile. Supported devices and operating system versions will be listed on each store page and on this site when they are confirmed.
